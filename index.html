Who We Are

Craftsmanship You Can Walk On

Roma Tile is a family-owned and operated tile and stone supplier serving Windsor, Essex County, and the surrounding region. We believe that the surfaces beneath your feet should be as beautiful as they are durable.

From porcelain and ceramic to natural stone, marble, and glass mosaic, our showroom features thousands of options sourced from the world's leading manufacturers. Whether you're renovating a single room or designing a custom home, our knowledgeable team is here to guide you every step of the way.

Porcelain & Ceramic
